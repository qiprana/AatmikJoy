HEALTH & HEALING · REFLECTION

What Is Health and Healing?

Healing is not just curing a disease. It is restoring harmony.

Healing is the process by which the body, mind, and inner system:

  • Return to balance
  • Restore function
  • Regain integrity
  • Re-establish self-regulation

It is not just curing a disease. It is restoring harmony.

Many paths, a shared intention

When we look back in time, humans have explored various healing modalities — be it acupuncture in regions of China, Ayurveda in India, Greek medicine, Egyptian medicine, Shamanism, Unani, Yoga, Marma, reflexology, Reiki, Pranic healing, Qigong, and spiritual healing, to name a few.

Healing at all levels of this human system — mind, body, senses, emotions, intellect, and spirit — is the true essence of healing that these modalities are gearing towards. Since this is such a complex mechanism, over the course of human evolution, many modalities have been gifted to the world, and each has its own unique place.

No matter what path we follow, the human species is looking for this union with the higher self that brings healing, strength, clarity, purpose, and meaning to this life on planet Earth. Each of us is unique in who we are, and there is an inner need to connect with what resonates with us. Sometimes multiple modalities, sometimes a single one, and sometimes one after another. Healing can come in any form and at any time. Let’s not give up.

Looking beyond symptoms

Sometimes it is deep inner work, trauma work, or body cleansing; sometimes it is just a simple shift in lifestyle or diet. When a human being is challenged with health, why stop at addressing the symptoms when the human body’s intelligence is far beyond what we can comprehend? Why not use these ancient sciences to explore root causes and find better ways of caring for this mind–body complex?

Say we have given ourselves unhelpful inputs — food, water, breath, or perception — for a long period of time. When the body–mind complex begins seeking attention, holding humble, compassionate space for this intelligent system to restore harmony is key. Sometimes it might be quick; often it is a slow and gradual process, influenced by how we live and what is conducive to this mind–body complex. The key is to be persistent.

Learning from the natural world

We humans are one among millions of species on this planet. When other species know how to thrive in their environments without a doctor or vaidya guiding them, where have humans lost this innate intelligence? Each species thrives in its own environment; when that environment is disturbed, crises happen. The same may be true for the human species.

Healing as an integrative journey

The bottom line is that healing can start at any point, through any modality. It is cumulative and can begin in any layer of the human being — mind, body, senses, emotions, intellect, or spirit — or perhaps in all of them. It is integrative. Health and healing matter, whatever modality or path we choose.

Which modality should we choose, and where do we begin? It starts with the inner calling and the paths that cross ours.

The key is to be persistent, compassionate, and open to the path that resonates with us.

This reflection explores a personal, integrative perspective on healing. It does not replace professional diagnosis, treatment, or support. Different healing modalities have different levels of evidence and should not be assumed to be equivalent or substitutes for necessary medical care.

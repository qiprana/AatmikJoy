Yoga - What are we identified with?

When we speak of Yoga, what are we truly identifying with?

The word Yoga is so widely used which translates to “Asana - yoga poses” to majority of us. Although we know the root work Yuj means Union, to Join - but what does it really mean?

Lets explore how we are using the word and what are we identifying with?

What yoga are you practicing? Its a very common question we face everyday when “Yoga” comes up. When we say “I practice Patanjali Yoga” which ideally translates to union with Sage Patanjali’s teachings, which is to unite with the Self through Astanga Yoga. But we instead get identified with the name or the concepts and forget where its leading us to (Union).

How about Hatha Yoga? Union through balance of Hatha (sun and moon). Iyengar Yoga? Hot Yoga? Vinyasa Yoga - dynamic flow of body and breath to find union? Bhakti Yoga - union with the Self with Bhakti, Karma Yoga through action…..

Is it identification with the practices or the flow of asanas or structure or concept or type of breath work or the person or is it with the Self?

Each found their path to unity and when shared with the world it got coined with a name. And people who resonated with that style started using “I practice “-” Yoga” and eventually created a much stronger identity with it instead of the Self. But Isnt’ the taste and smell of unity of the Self the same? Asana is one path to taste this unity but there are 100’s many more. All of them are Yoga.

Expressions of Yoga

  • Dexterity in action is Yoga
  • Yoga is the cessation of the fluctuations of the mind.
  • Yoga = inner stillness + self-realization
  • Yoga is equanimity (balance of mind).
  • Yoga is acting without attachment to results.
  • Yoga = union with the Divine (Īśvara / Brahman)

May be its time to rewire our brains to think of Unity and Union with the Self as Yoga (Mind, Body emotions, senses and soul), a personal inner relationship and journey that brings all our parts into harmony and unity. If this is what it truly means then how long should we practice yoga everyday to bring in harmony with ourself? Answer is 24/7 🙂.

What we speak is in harmony with what we think is in harmony with what we act in harmony with what we feel in harmony with inner voice and inner calling.

May be we can take a minute to pause and connect with ourselves next time we hear Yoga. Inner Joy of Being. Yoga is the process of bringing the mind-body-senses-emotions-Soul into union (Samyoga). Also means centeredness which brings about connection with ourselves at all planes of consciousness (Physical, mental, emotional, intellectual and spiritual). Building awareness is Yoga. How we get there is an exploration and a journey and many many paths and different names!

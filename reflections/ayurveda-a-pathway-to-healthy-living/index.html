Ayurveda - a pathway to healthy living

Why do we need to live long? What does it mean to live a healthy life?

Why do we need to live long? Or when do we feel we want to have a long life? Do we even aspire to live long in this modern rat race? Some common questions we tend to introspect.

When we have a healthy body, enthusiastic mind, connect to our inner purpose, and the aatmikJoy bubbling through us, dont we want to live as long as possible to live and expereince the life fully given in front of us? It even feels life is too short in that state of mind-body. Our Soul does pick a mission to this life to accomplish be it learning to handle anger or build a bridge or to plant trees or play piano or take care of the family what ever life shows us we need a healthy vehicle to navigate through this life.

So how do we have a healthy life? What does health even mean? How does it look like? How does it taste and smell? We are stuck with not knowing what to eat, how to eat, when to sleep, how to exercise, how much to drink, how to handle emotions, how to connect with ourselves, what is source of our joy, etc etc. How were we to know about all of these? If we “A Human Species” is designed to birth on the planet, but then dont we need a User Manual on how to operate and take care of this mind-body complex. This is what is Ayurveda all about.

Its a

  • Science of healing body and mind
  • Way of living
  • Science of longitivtiy, how to live a healthy life
  • Connecting with the nature, who you are truly made of and where you came from
  • You are the healer within.
  • It is a Health User Manual given to us from our sages
  • You are a unique combination of 5 elements (Air, Ether, Fire, Water, Earth) and functional energies Vata(movement), Pitta(Transformation), Kapha(Coherence, Binding)
  • You are not what you eat, but what you digest.

Its not about

  • Eating kichidi
  • Taking herbs and be done
  • Do panchakarma and be done.
  • Its an old science and does not apply to modern life
  • Its born in India and does not apply to everyone

Ayurveda is an endless ocean of exploration and a definite path to wellbeing and longitivtiy!
